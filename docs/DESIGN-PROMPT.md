# ALAS Salt Co. — website design prompt

Paste everything below the line into an AI design or website tool, or hand it
to a designer. It describes the site as decided so far. Where something is
still undecided it says so, so the tool doesn't fill the gap with a guess.

---

You are designing the website for **ALAS Salt Co.**, a premium US brand
selling 100% Aegean mineral sea salt, hand-harvested in Greece and packaged in
the USA. "Άλας" is Greek for salt. The products are salt pouches and kits,
single-serve Hydration Sticks, and gift boxes, sold online in USD to US
customers. Shopify runs the products, cart and checkout; the site displays
them.

## The feeling

Dark, editorial, premium direct-to-consumer. Calm, confident, restrained.
Reference points for the level of polish: **im8health.com** and
**drinkag1.com**. Think of a luxury wellness brand with Aegean roots: deep
sea navy, white type, generous space, few elements, nothing shouting. It
should feel crafted and timeless, never like a supplement-store template.
The company's own ambition is to be "the Hermès of hydration": luxury
ritual, not sports nutrition. (That phrase is a tone reference only and
never appears on the site.)

The salt is a Greek **fleur de sel**. The audience runs from athletes,
endurance runners in particular, to everyday wellness enthusiasts.

Mostly navy with white type. Light sections are the exception, not the rhythm.

## Typography

- **Headings:** an elegant, high-contrast serif. The licensed display face is
  not settled yet ("The Seasons" or "Dahlia Medium"), so use a refined serif
  as a stand-in and keep it swappable.
- **Body and interface:** Inter.
- Headings tightly tracked: −0.03em, and −0.06em at display sizes.
- Small uppercase eyebrow labels above headings, widely tracked at +0.3em
  (e.g. "ANCIENT ELEMENTS OF THE AEGEAN").
- Body text is mostly 14–16px. Hero headline is large, about 72px on desktop.

## Layout and components

- Content max width about 1640px, with generous vertical spacing between
  sections.
- Corner radius 8–10px on cards and images; buttons are full pills.
- Primary buttons are solid white with navy uppercase tracked text. Secondary
  buttons are white outline pills.
- The logo is a **stacked lockup** (ALAS over SALT CO), white on navy, sized
  by height. Don't redraw or restyle it; it's the brand's mark.
- Header: logo left; Shop, Account, Cart right. A thin black announcement bar
  above it reads "FREE SHIPPING ANYWHERE IN THE USA".

## Motion

- The hero is a dark sea surface that **moves slowly**: a gentle swell and a
  drifting light sheen, almost subliminal, never busy.
- Ambition, not yet built: **3D-style product moments**, such as a spoon
  scooping salt, salt pouring into a glass of water, salt pouring out of the
  pouch. These must show the real ALAS packaging, not an invented one.
- All motion stops for visitors who have turned on "reduce motion" in their
  device settings.

## Other pages

- **Shop**: a grid of all products.
- **Product page**: large gallery on the left. On the right: title, price,
  a size selector (pouch counts for salt; 5 / 15 / 30 sticks for the
  Hydration Sticks), a **flavour selector** (Regular, Blueberry, Lemon) on
  the salt pouches and the Hydration Sticks, quantity, Add to cart, then the
  description. Size and flavour should read as two simple rows of pill
  buttons, with the current choice clearly marked.
- **Cart**: line items on the left, summary card on the right, white Checkout
  pill.
- **Account**: sign in, register, profile, order history. Quiet, narrow,
  centred forms.
- **Contact**: a simple email-based page, not a form.

## Flavours

ALAS comes in three flavours: **Regular**, **Blueberry** and **Lemon**, on
both the **salt pouches** and the **Hydration Sticks**. The gift boxes don't
have a flavour choice. Show flavour as a choice on the product page, and
name the flavour on product cards and in the cart.

Two cautions:

- The current Hydration Sticks copy says "No sugar, flavours, or fillers",
  and the homepage criticises products that "rely on artificial flavours".
  Neither line may appear next to a flavoured product. New wording for the
  flavoured versions has to come from the owners.
- Don't describe the flavours as "natural", "sugar-free" or anything else
  about their ingredients until the owners confirm it.

## Rules: do not break these

- **Don't invent numbers.** No star ratings, review counts, customer counts,
  statistics, percentages or delivery times. Leave a clearly marked
  placeholder instead.
- **Don't write new health claims.** Use only the hydration and electrolyte
  wording above. Nothing about disease, diabetes, stroke, immunity, asthma,
  allergies or vitamins, and never "clinically proven".
- **Never write "Made in USA".** Write "harvested in Greece, packaged in the
  USA".
- **Don't write a founder story or an About bio.** That copy comes from the
  owners.
- **Prices come from the store.** Use the real prices below for mock-ups; don't
  invent sale prices or "was/now" pricing.
- **Don't lift claims from the investor deck.** It was written for
  investors, not customers. Its claims about bioavailability, cellular or
  mitochondrial health, longevity, being microplastic-free and zero
  contamination go beyond the site's approved wording, and its "Made and
  packaged in the USA" line breaks the rule above.
- **Imagery:** use real product photography only. No generic stock (shops,
  spa towels, coastline postcards) and no AI-generated product shots
  presented as real.
- **Accessibility:** text contrast at least WCAG AA, alt text on every
  image, everything usable by keyboard, and motion that respects "reduce
  motion".

## Still undecided: leave these as placeholders

- The display typeface
- Brand spelling: the store says "Álas", the packaging says "ALAS"
- Founder and About copy
- Real product photography to replace the placeholders
- Final kit prices, and whether "Save up to 20%" stays (the new subscription
  price works out at 40% off)
- What goes into each flavour (the flavours themselves are confirmed)
- **The weight of one Hydration Stick.** The owners say 1 g; the printed
  stick and the store both say 2 g. Show stick counts, not grams, until it's
  settled.
- **The trace-mineral count.** The site says "80+", the supplier's data says
  "up to 84", and the investor deck also says "72+". It must become one
  number.
- **What's in the Welcome Box.** Three different contents lists are in
  circulation. Don't list its contents until one is confirmed.
